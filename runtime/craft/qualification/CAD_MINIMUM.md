# CAD minimum with measured editable geometry

The maintained `craft/runtime/tools/cad_acceptance.py` fixture exercises all nine
public CAD operations on the sealed macOS candidate. It uses independent geometric
expectations and actual native OBJ files, with a separate headless Blender reader.
This is bounded metric-panel evidence, not general CAD or manufacturing approval.

The completed run passes **89 checks across 34 durable jobs**: 26
successes, 8 expected worker refusals and 3 separate admission refusals.
The worker commands take **94.470 seconds** in total, including package
verification and validation. This is local wall time, not CPU time, peak memory,
energy, cloud cost or total qualification elapsed time.

## Observed behavior

A 120 × 80 × 12 mm panel retains an editable constrained sketch and native solid
history. Width and height change to 150 × 90 mm, followed by explicit solving and
20 mm extrusion. A transfer to another private state changes depth to 8 mm, then
restores the original dimensions and byte-identical original OBJ delivery.

- Parameter edits change only the named expressions. Geometry remains unchanged
  and solve/extrusion claims become pending. Delivery refuses unsolved geometry.
- Native solving moves only the four constrained lines, retains their fixed
  origin and keeps the old mesh unchanged. Independent endpoints stay within
  0.000001 mm of requested corners. The old mesh cannot be delivered as revised.
- Explicit solid regeneration retains mesh identity, entity order and unrelated
  model state. Complete predictions constrain vertices and the depth expression.
- An independent OBJ parser checks actual coordinates, units, all eight unique
  corners, six outward quad faces, twelve oppositely paired manifold edges,
  volume and surface area. Native source coordinates/topology equal delivery.
  Repeated cold exports are byte-identical. Small and large admitted dimensions
  are also measured, without extending the manufacturing claim.
- Inspection, bundling, native JSON and standalone archive imports preserve exact
  originals and complete models. Whole-project transfer remains editable; reverse
  revisions recover the original OBJ without rewriting the original source.
- Line, circle and Unicode text authoring exercises all primitive revision kinds,
  including stable handles and entity properties. Complete unrelated drawing
  state remains equal. Invalid batches, stale expectations and unsupported
  production publish no revision. Invalid dimensions, unsupported DXF import and
  cross-project assets are refused at admission.
- Reconnecting MCP clients discover the same nine CAD operations and retrieve the
  same durable result. Database integrity, process-group cleanup, checkout
  receipts and the entire package seal are checked.

`bb7675ba05e2c3c4bdc824c83110d250ab67745a57d546a58143e8123f1912d0`.
The retained migration log records thirty passing checks across five cases.

Deterministic reconstruction from the pinned baseline and original 120 × 80 mm

the original file survived cleanup or that build4 was executed again in this unit.
The recovered archive is bound to its recorded historical observation, rather

predecessor, original, complete model and history prefix. Native solid production
then creates an OBJ identical to the original panel. Repeated migration is refused.

## Independent scene delivery

Blender 5.3.0 Beta independently imports three actual OBJ deliveries, measures
their dimensions, manifold topology, outward normals, volume and area, and saves
editable `.blend` scenes plus 1200 × 900 PNG inspection images. It is an independent
reader here; the native CAD engine authors the solids. All three scenes use the
same oblique orthographic camera and neutral inspection appearance. No external
images or paid model service are required.

New Blender processes reopen each saved scene with scripting disabled, preserve
its units, camera and measured mesh, and render pixel-identical PNGs. The source
OBJ and saved scene bytes remain unchanged. Assistant inspection confirms visible
front/side faces and uncropped geometry across the three variants; owner visual
acceptance remains open. These are Workbench geometry inspections, not realistic
material photographs or manufacturing certification.

The three independent Blender import/render processes take 3.193, 1.389
and 1.574 seconds of wall time, with maximum resident sizes of 374,079,488,
400,867,328 and 398,737,408 bytes respectively. These host measurements include
process startup; they do not measure total GPU memory or predict photographic
scene cost. The separate cold-reopen runs are additional work.
